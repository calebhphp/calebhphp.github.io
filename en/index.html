Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know anything about tech?

No. You tell me how your business works and I handle the rest. I'll only show you what you'll use, in plain language.

How long does it take?

A digital card takes 2 days, a digital menu 3, a landing page 5 and a website 7 to 10 days. Bigger projects are delivered in weekly milestones.

How does payment work?

You get a fixed price before we start. Usually half upfront and half on delivery, or by milestone for larger projects. Payment by card, PayPal, Wise or bank transfer.

Will I own the website and domain?

Yes. Everything I build is yours: domain, content and access.

You're in Brazil. Does that work for me?

Yes. I worked for over two years with a team in Germany. We talk by WhatsApp, email or video call, in English, and I overlap with US and European business hours.

What if I need help later?

Every project includes 30 days of free tweaks. After that, you can stay on a care plan or just reach out when you need something.
